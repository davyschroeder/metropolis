Metro Sign (metrosign-regular.woff, metrosign-bold.woff)

The interface typeface of METROPOLIS. These two files are Latin subsets of TeX Gyre Heros Condensed
Regular and Bold (GUST e-foundry, B. Jackowski and J. M. Nowacki, based on URW Nimbus Sans L), converted
to WOFF and renamed as the GUST Font License asks of derived works. The licence is in
GUST-FONT-LICENSE.txt; the original fonts are at https://www.gust.org.pl/projects/e-foundry/tex-gyre
